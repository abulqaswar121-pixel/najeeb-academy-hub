### 1. Course Overview & Target Audience
Ghostwriting on LinkedIn requires capturing an executive's authentic voice, extracting proprietary insights, and packaging them into high-converting posts. This course teaches how to build and operate a multi-agent AI ghostwriting system that automates research, drafting, style compliance, and client revisions while keeping human editorial judgement at the core.

**Who This Course Is For:**
- Freelance writers and copywriters transitioning into high-ticket executive ghostwriting.
- Content marketers and agency operators looking to scale LinkedIn content production without sacrificing authenticity.
- Founders and executives who want to build an internal AI-assisted personal branding engine.
- Learners who want to master no-code AI agent workflows and voice replication frameworks.

**What You Will Produce:**
- A configured multi-agent LinkedIn ghostwriting architecture with research, drafting, and feedback nodes.
- A comprehensive Executive Voice Dossier and tonality rulebook for a target founder.
- A 4-week, 12-post thought leadership campaign ready for client delivery.
- An automated Slack/chat review interface for real-time post iterations.

### 5. Structured Lesson Breakdown (6 Lessons)
#### Lesson 1: Introduction to AI Ghostwriting & Architecture
- **Timestamp:** `00:00` - `05:00` (0s to 300s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Explores the fundamental shift from manual freelance writing to AI-augmented ghostwriting. The lesson deconstructs why single-prompt LLM generation produces generic, easily detectable LinkedIn content. It introduces the multi-agent paradigm: decoupling information gathering, draft synthesis, and voice tuning into distinct computational steps to preserve founder authenticity.
- **Practical Activity:** Map out a 3-agent ghostwriting workflow on paper or digital canvas (Researcher -> Drafter -> Critic) and identify the specific inputs required at each stage.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** Why do single-prompt LinkedIn posts almost always sound like generic marketing copy, and how does multi-agent separation solve this?

#### Lesson 2: Live Ghostwriting Agent Demonstration & Output Inspection
- **Timestamp:** `05:00` - `10:00` (300s to 600s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Demonstrates a functioning ghostwriting agent running live. Analyzes the differences between raw LLM output and agentic output conditioned on specific founder voice parameters. The lesson highlights key evaluation criteria: hook strength, line spacing, conversational rhythm, story-to-insight ratio, and call-to-conversation endings.
- **Practical Activity:** Take a sample generic AI-generated LinkedIn post and manually annotate every instance of generic phrasing, passive voice, or artificial buzzwords.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** What are the three most noticeable 'AI tells' on LinkedIn, and what specific rules can prevent them?

#### Lesson 3: Building the Topic & Industry Research Agent
- **Timestamp:** `10:00` - `25:15` (600s to 1515s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Step-by-step build of the Research Agent. Covers scraping niche newsletters, parsing RSS feeds, extracting trending news from industry portals, and identifying content gaps. Shows how to engineer system instructions that force the agent to extract specific data points, contrarian angles, and actionable case studies rather than surface-level summaries.
- **Practical Activity:** Build and test the system prompt for a Research Agent targeting a specific industry (e.g., B2B SaaS or FinTech) and verify that it outputs 5 concrete story angles.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** How does providing primary research data improve the Information Gain score of an AI-assisted post?

#### Lesson 4: Configuring the Copywriting & Tone-Feedback Agent
- **Timestamp:** `25:15` - `38:30` (1515s to 2310s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Focuses on the core drafting and feedback loops. Implements the Executive Voice Matrix: sentence length distribution, vocabulary constraints, preferred hook templates (e.g., 'Contrarian Take', 'Behind-the-Scenes Breakdown', 'Hard Truth'), and paragraph structures. Integrates a Critic Agent that scores drafts against brand guidelines before human review.
- **Practical Activity:** Configure a Critic Agent prompt that scores drafts from 1-10 on Voice Match, Hook Punchiness, and Jargon Absence, with mandatory rewrite suggestions.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** Why is a dedicated critique step more reliable than asking the drafter agent to 'check its own work' in a single prompt?

#### Lesson 5: Creating a Slack Assistant for Instant AI Drafting & Editing
- **Timestamp:** `38:30` - `45:00` (2310s to 2700s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Connects the AI agent backend to Slack using webhook triggers and conversational bots. Demonstrates how a ghostwriter or founder can drop a 30-second voice note, rough bullet points, or an article link into Slack and receive three polished, tone-aligned LinkedIn drafts within seconds, complete with revision options.
- **Practical Activity:** Configure a webhook or test harness that accepts a raw bullet-point input and returns two formatted LinkedIn draft variations.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** How does lowering the friction of capturing founder thoughts in real time increase the quality of ghostwritten content?

#### Lesson 6: Advanced Workflow Optimization, Quality Control & Client Delivery
- **Timestamp:** `45:00` - `61:33` (2700s to 3693s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Covers production operations, client onboarding, batch scheduling, and continuous feedback loops. Teaches how to conduct a 45-minute monthly interview that fuels an entire month of content, how to present drafts in a client-friendly approval portal, and how to track engagement metrics to refine the agent prompts over time.
- **Practical Activity:** Draft a complete Client Onboarding Voice Questionnaire containing 10 deep-dive extraction questions designed to uncover personal stories and contrarian views.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** What role does human editorial judgement play when AI handles 80% of the initial research and drafting workload?

### 6. Written Learning Materials & Quality Assurance
**Actionable Verification Checklist:**
- [ ] Target founder persona and voice rules codified in a dedicated prompt file.
- [ ] Research agent configured with trusted industry RSS and news sources.
- [ ] Drafting agent loaded with 5 high-performing sample posts as few-shot examples.
- [ ] Critic agent configured with explicit negative constraints (no buzzwords, no emojis in headers).
- [ ] Automated trigger (webhook, Slack, or form) tested end-to-end.
- [ ] Human editorial review protocol established prior to client publication.

**Common Pitfalls & Fixes:**
- **Mistake:** Over-indexing on generic AI formatting
  - **Correction:** Relying on numbered lists with emoji bullet points on every line. Fix: Force conversational paragraph flow and varied sentence structures.
- **Mistake:** Ignoring founder vocabulary
  - **Correction:** Allowing the AI to use words the founder never speaks in real life. Fix: Maintain a strict 'Banned Words' and 'Signature Vocabulary' dictionary.
- **Mistake:** Failing to include personal anecdotes
  - **Correction:** Publishing textbook advice without personal context. Fix: Always ground posts in a specific client scenario, failure, or real metric.
- **Mistake:** Skipping the human review step
  - **Correction:** Publishing raw AI output directly to executive profiles. Fix: Enforce a mandatory 5-minute human polishing pass for cadence and fact-checking.
