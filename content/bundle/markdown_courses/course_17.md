### 1. Course Overview & Target Audience
Modern podcasting has been revolutionized by AI text-based editing, voice cloning, automated noise removal, and smart video clipping. This course covers the complete production lifecycle in Descript: ingesting raw recordings, generating AI transcripts, editing audio via text, applying Studio Sound, creating animated captions, training AI voice clones for audio patch repairs, and exporting multitrack stems.

**Who This Course Is For:**
- Podcasters, audio producers, and YouTube creators wanting to cut editing time by 75%.
- Content marketing teams turning long-form executive interviews into viral social clips.
- Freelance audio/video editors upgrading their toolset to AI-first production platforms.
- Beginners launching a new audio or video podcast with zero prior DAW experience.

**What You Will Produce:**
- A fully produced 20-30 minute audio and video podcast episode with custom intro/outro.
- Three vertical short-form video clips (9:16) with dynamic animated captions and visual b-roll.
- A trained AI voice clone model used for seamless Overdub audio patch replacements.
- A complete Show Notes package with timestamps, summaries, and speaker-attributed transcripts.

### 6. Written Learning Materials & Quality Assurance
**Actionable Verification Checklist:**
- [ ] Multitrack files imported and synchronized.
- [ ] AI transcript generated and speaker names labeled.
- [ ] Filler words and awkward pauses trimmed.
- [ ] Studio Sound applied with acoustic balance verified.
- [ ] Overdub patches created for misspoken words.
- [ ] Three 9:16 vertical clips created with dynamic captions.
- [ ] Audio mastered to -16 LUFS and exported as WAV/MP3.

**Common Pitfalls & Fixes:**
- **Mistake:** Leaving Studio Sound on 100%
  - **Correction:** Causes robotic phasing and muffled highs. Fix: Set intensity to 75-85% for natural vocal presence.
- **Mistake:** Over-cutting natural speech rhythm
  - **Correction:** Removing every micro-pause making dialogue sound unnatural. Fix: Keep natural 0.3-0.5s pauses between sentences.
- **Mistake:** Neglecting transcript proofreading
  - **Correction:** Allowing AI spelling errors into on-screen captions. Fix: Always do a text search for names, brands, and technical terms.
- **Mistake:** Exporting clips with incorrect aspect ratios
  - **Correction:** Posting 16:9 video to TikTok. Fix: Use Descript's composition aspect ratio switcher to create native 9:16 clips.
