### 6. Written Learning Materials & Quality Assurance
**Actionable Verification Checklist:**

- [ ] 90-day growth roadmap with 12 new video packaging concepts finalized.

**Common Pitfalls & Fixes:**
- **Mistake:** Focusing exclusively on tags and search SEO
  - **Correction:** Obsessing over keyword tags when 80%+ of views come from Browse and Suggested feeds. Fix: Focus on thumbnail packaging and broad emotional hooks.
- **Mistake:** Auditing only views while ignoring CTR and AVD
  - **Correction:** Assuming low views means a bad topic when it was simply a weak thumbnail. Fix: Always isolate CTR (packaging problem) from AVD (content problem).
- **Mistake:** Posting unrelated content topics
  - **Correction:** Confusing the algorithm by switching between gaming, cooking, and crypto. Fix: Stick to 3 tightly aligned content pillars.
- **Mistake:** Failing to update underperforming thumbnails on old videos
  - **Correction:** Leaving 2% CTR thumbnails on evergreen videos. Fix: A/B test and replace old thumbnails to revive dormant videos.
