### 6. Written Learning Materials & Quality Assurance
**Actionable Verification Checklist:**
- [ ] Video premise directly supports thumbnail and title promise.
- [ ] Hook establishes core question and stakes within 10 seconds.
- [ ] At least 3 open loops planted before the halfway mark.
- [ ] Micro-payoffs delivered every 90-120 seconds.
- [ ] POWER editing pass completed with script read aloud.
- [ ] 2-column storyboard populated with visual and sound effects cues.

**Common Pitfalls & Fixes:**
- **Mistake:** Writing long introductory throat-clearing
  - **Correction:** Spending 30 seconds saying 'Welcome back to my channel!'. Fix: Start immediately with the core hook and promise.
- **Mistake:** Writing for the eye instead of the ear
  - **Correction:** Using academic, overly complex sentence structures. Fix: Write in short, punchy 8-12 word conversational sentences.
- **Mistake:** Failing to include visual directions
  - **Correction:** Delivering a wall of text with no B-roll cues. Fix: Write visual directions for every single paragraph of spoken dialogue.
- **Mistake:** Giving away the payoff too early
  - **Correction:** Answering the core mystery in minute 1. Fix: Tease the answer, explore the stakes, and deliver the payoff at peak emotional tension.
