## Course 20: AI Social Media Management
**Track:** Marketing & Growth | **Pricing:** NGN 25,000 / $35 USD
**Cover Image:** `cover_images/course_20_cover.jpg`

### 1. Course Overview & Target Audience
Social Media Management (SMM) has shifted from manual scheduling and generic posting to AI-driven multi-platform content engines. This course teaches how to build and operate a modern social media agency or in-house SMM workflow from zero capital: leveraging AI for high-velocity content production, multi-format repurposing, automated client prospecting, and systematic client onboarding.

**Who This Course Is For:**
- Aspiring agency owners and freelancers starting a Social Media Marketing Agency (SMMA).
- Social media managers wanting to automate content drafting, visual assets, and monthly reporting.
- Brand marketing managers handling multi-channel publishing (LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, X).
- Beginners looking for practical, zero-cost business acquisition frameworks.

**What You Will Produce:**
- A 30-day multi-channel social media content calendar (60+ posts across 4 platforms).
- An AI-powered Content Batching Pipeline with custom prompt libraries for hooks, captions, and carousel slides.
- A Client Prospecting & Outreach System with personalized cold pitch templates.
- A Client Onboarding & Service Level Agreement (SLA) operational pack.

### 5. Structured Lesson Breakdown (6 Lessons)
#### Lesson 1: Modern Social Media Ecosystem & AI Opportunity
- **Timestamp:** `00:00` - `01:03` (0s to 63s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Introduces the modern social media landscape. Analyzes why traditional manual agency models fail due to high labor overhead and how AI tools allow a solo operator to deliver the creative output of a 5-person agency team with zero initial software costs.
- **Practical Activity:** Audit the social media presence of two local businesses, identifying their posting frequency, visual consistency, and engagement rates.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** Why is consistency and multi-format testing the primary driver of organic social media growth?

#### Lesson 2: Core Mechanics of Social Media Marketing & Channel Architecture
- **Timestamp:** `01:03` - `02:25` (63s to 145s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Deconstructs the specific algorithmic mechanics of LinkedIn (professional insights, carousels), Instagram (Reels, aesthetic carousels), X (threads, contrarian thoughts), and TikTok (fast hooks, trending audio). Establishes how to build distinct content pillars for a brand.
- **Practical Activity:** Develop 4 distinct content pillars for a selected business niche, defining the goal and format for each pillar.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** Why must the same core message be adapted into fundamentally different formats for LinkedIn vs Instagram?

#### Lesson 3: High-Value AI SMM Skills: Content Planning, Creation & Analytics
- **Timestamp:** `02:25` - `06:28` (145s to 388s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Deep dive into batch content generation with AI. Demonstrates prompt workflows for turning 1 core topic into 10 multi-platform posts: generating carousel text slides, punchy caption hooks, hashtag research, and community conversation starters.
- **Practical Activity:** Prompt an AI model to batch-generate 10 LinkedIn posts and 10 Instagram carousel scripts from a single case study.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** How does batching content 30 days in advance protect the agency from creative burnout and client panic?

#### Lesson 4: Client Outreach, Proposal Systems & Business Prospecting
- **Timestamp:** `06:28` - `08:40` (388s to 520s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Comprehensive guide to landing social media clients with $0 ad spend. Teaches prospecting via Google Maps, Instagram DM audits, cold email frameworks, and recorded video audits that pinpoint specific flaws in a prospect's social strategy.
- **Practical Activity:** Build a prospecting list of 10 qualified businesses and draft a personalized video audit outreach script.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** Why is offering a specific visual audit far more effective than sending generic 'I can manage your social media' pitch messages?

#### Lesson 5: The 'Sink or Swim' Client Onboarding & Service Delivery Protocol
- **Timestamp:** `08:40` - `11:59` (520s to 719s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Walks through the operational onboarding sprint: setting up shared Notion dashboards, collecting brand assets, defining approval protocols, establishing KPI baselines, and scheduling the first 14 days of content before public launch.
- **Practical Activity:** Create a Client Onboarding Notion Template containing brand asset checklists, login vaults, and approval workflows.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** What are the common communication breakdowns during the first week with a new client, and how does a structured SOP prevent them?

#### Lesson 6: Campaign Escalation, Reporting & Long-Term Account Growth
- **Timestamp:** `11:59` - `16:28` (719s to 988s)

- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** How does demonstrating direct business impact (inquiries, revenue) protect your agency retainer during economic downturns?

### 6. Written Learning Materials & Quality Assurance
**Actionable Verification Checklist:**
- [ ] Brand identity, tone guidelines, and 4 content pillars defined.
- [ ] 30-day content calendar drafted across at least 3 platforms.
- [ ] Visual templates created in Canva/Figma.
- [ ] Scheduled queue loaded in Metricool/Buffer.
- [ ] Cold outreach audit script tested on 5 prospects.
- [ ] Client onboarding Notion dashboard configured.

**Common Pitfalls & Fixes:**
- **Mistake:** Posting without visual templates
  - **Correction:** Creating disjointed graphics with inconsistent fonts and colors. Fix: Build 5 reusable Canva/Figma master brand templates.
- **Mistake:** Writing generic captions without hooks
  - **Correction:** Starting posts with 'Here is our latest update'. Fix: Use pattern-interrupting hooks on line 1.
- **Mistake:** Failing to engage in comments
  - **Correction:** Publishing posts and closing the app immediately. Fix: Spend 15 minutes engaging with commenters right after publishing.
- **Mistake:** Sending spammy cold outreach
  - **Correction:** Copy-pasting identical messages to 100 businesses. Fix: Personalize every pitch with a specific observation from their current profile.
