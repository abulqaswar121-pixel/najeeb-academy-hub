### 1. Course Overview & Target Audience
Modern photography retouching has evolved from tedious hours of manual frequency separation and stamp cloning to AI-assisted workflows that deliver magazine-grade portraits in seconds while preserving authentic skin texture. This course covers the complete workflow for AI skin smoothing, glare elimination, garment wrinkle removal, and commercial image finishing.

**Who This Course Is For:**
- Portrait and commercial photographers wanting to reduce editing turnaround times from days to hours.
- Photo editors and retouchers looking to integrate generative AI tools into Photoshop and Lightroom workflows.
- Content creators, agencies, and e-commerce brands producing high-volume product and model imagery.
- Beginners who want to learn professional skin retouching principles without getting lost in complex manual masks.

**What You Will Produce:**
- A 5-image commercial portrait retouching portfolio demonstrating skin smoothing, glare removal, and fabric cleanup.
- A customized AI Retouching Action/Preset workflow for high-volume batch processing.
- A non-destructive Photoshop/Lightroom master file archive with layer-based adjustment stacks.
- A client delivery package with high-resolution web, print, and social exports.

### 5. Structured Lesson Breakdown (5 Lessons)
#### Lesson 1: Introduction to Modern AI Retouching Workflows
- **Timestamp:** `00:00` - `00:14` (0s to 14s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Introduces the paradigm shift in digital photography post-production. Compares traditional multi-hour manual retouching methods with modern AI-accelerated techniques. Establishes the core philosophy of professional AI retouching: AI should be used as a precision accelerator to handle repetitive pixel manipulation while human artistic direction controls final texture, realism, and aesthetic consistency.
- **Practical Activity:** Import three unretouched RAW portraits into your editing workspace and inspect them at 100% zoom to identify key areas requiring texture preservation versus smoothing.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** Why is the preservation of authentic skin texture the single most critical standard separating professional AI retouching from amateur AI filters?

#### Lesson 2: AI Skin Retouching & Natural Texture Smoothing
- **Timestamp:** `00:14` - `02:33` (14s to 153s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Step-by-step masterclass on AI skin smoothing. Demonstrates how AI neural algorithms detect facial landmarks, analyze skin tones, and smooth out blotchiness, redness, and blemishes without erasing natural skin pores. Teaches how to adjust AI smoothing intensity, mask out sensitive facial zones (eyes, lips, nostrils), and blend the AI layer with the underlying base image.
- **Practical Activity:** Apply AI skin smoothing to a portrait, adjust the effect intensity to 60-70%, and paint a layer mask to keep the eyes and lips completely sharp.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** How does layer blending and opacity control prevent the over-processed 'plastic mannequin' look common in automated retouching?

#### Lesson 3: AI-Powered Glare & Reflection Removal
- **Timestamp:** `02:33` - `05:16` (153s to 316s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Focuses on one of the hardest manual retouching challenges: removing harsh reflections and studio light glare from eyeglasses. Demonstrates how generative inpainting analyzes the visible iris and eyelid structure behind the glass to reconstruct the eye naturally while eliminating reflective flare and color fringing.
- **Practical Activity:** Select a portrait with eyeglass glare, use precision lasso selection around the glare artifact, and execute AI inpainting to reconstruct the eye naturally.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** What visual cues must be verified after inpainting eyeglasses to ensure the subject's gaze and eye symmetry remain anatomically correct?

#### Lesson 4: Automated Garment Wrinkle Removal & Fabric Cleanup
- **Timestamp:** `05:16` - `08:21` (316s to 501s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Covers commercial wardrobe and fabric retouching. Demonstrates using generative AI to smooth deep creases, iron out distracting fabric wrinkles on shirts and suits, and remove lint/dust particles without destroying the natural weave and structure of the textile.
- **Practical Activity:** Select a corporate headshot with wrinkled clothing, apply AI fabric smoothing across the shoulders and chest, and verify that the garment seams remain intact.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** Why is wardrobe cleanup essential in commercial and corporate headshot photography, and how does AI make it commercially viable?

#### Lesson 5: Master Export, Visual QA, and Color Consistency
- **Timestamp:** `08:21` - `11:14` (501s to 674s)
- **Summary & Notes:** Final assembly, quality assurance, and commercial delivery. Covers reviewing the full image stack at 100% and 200% magnification, checking for edge artifacts or unnatural blur halos, applying global color grading and sharpening, and exporting high-bitrate master files formatted for print (CMYK/AdobeRGB) and web (sRGB).
- **Practical Activity:** Perform a complete 5-point QA inspection on your finished portrait and export high-resolution master deliverables for both web and print.
- **Knowledge Check / Reflection:** What are the final quality checks required before delivering an AI-retouched image to a commercial client?

### 6. Written Learning Materials & Quality Assurance
**Actionable Verification Checklist:**
- [ ] Original RAW file imported and base exposure balanced.
- [ ] AI skin smoothing applied with texture preservation verified at 100% zoom.
- [ ] Eyeglass glare and specular distractions cleanly removed.
- [ ] Wardrobe wrinkles smoothed while maintaining fabric weave.
- [ ] Global color grading applied across the entire image series.
- [ ] Deliverables exported in correct color spaces (sRGB for web, AdobeRGB for print).

**Common Pitfalls & Fixes:**
- **Mistake:** Over-smoothing skin into plastic
  - **Correction:** Setting AI smoothing slider to 100%. Fix: Dial back opacity to 60-75% and inspect pore detail.
- **Mistake:** Distorting eye anatomy during glare removal
  - **Correction:** Letting AI hallucinate a different eye shape. Fix: Use smaller selection brushes and reference the second eye for symmetry.
- **Mistake:** Erasing garment seams
  - **Correction:** Selecting across structural cloth lines. Fix: Mask along seam boundaries to preserve garment structure.
- **Mistake:** Exporting in wrong color profiles
  - **Correction:** Delivering AdobeRGB to web clients causing dull, washed-out colors. Fix: Always convert to sRGB for digital delivery.
